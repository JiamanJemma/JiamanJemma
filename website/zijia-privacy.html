隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

紫甲 Zijia · 最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-03-12

概述

紫甲（Zijia）尊重你的隐私。本应用的设计原则是将数据留在你的设备上，我们不运营任何用于收集用户数据的服务器。

数据存储

  • 所有占卜记录、问题、设置等数据存储在你的设备本地（SwiftData）。
  • 如果你启用了 iCloud 同步，数据通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 在你自己的设备间同步。我们无法访问你的 iCloud 数据。
  • 每日一卦和 Widget 数据通过 App Groups 在应用与小组件之间共享，仅存储在你的设备上。

AI 解读服务

  • 当你选择"深度解读"时，你的问题和卦象信息会发送至第三方 AI 服务（DeepSeek）以生成解读内容。
  • 我们仅发送卦象数据和你主动输入的问题，不会附加任何设备标识符或个人信息。
  • AI 服务提供商有独立的隐私政策，请参阅其相关条款。

本地通知

  • 如果你开启"每日一卦提醒"，应用使用 iOS 本地通知功能在每天早上发送提醒。
  • 通知内容在你的设备上生成，不经过任何外部服务器。

权限使用

  • 运动传感器 — 仅用于检测摇动手势以进行占卜交互。传感器数据不会被存储或传输。
  • 通知 — 仅用于每日一卦提醒，可随时在设置中关闭。

第三方服务

  • 本应用不包含任何广告框架或第三方分析 SDK。
  • 订阅和购买通过 Apple 的 App Store 处理，受 Apple 隐私政策约束。

数据追踪

本应用不追踪你的任何活动。我们不使用 IDFA 或任何设备标识符进行追踪。

儿童隐私

本应用不针对 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

联系方式

如有任何隐私相关问题，请联系：jiamanjemma@gmail.com

Overview

Zijia (紫甲) respects your privacy. This app is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not operate any servers to collect user data.

Data Storage

  • All divination records, questions, and settings are stored locally on your device (SwiftData).
  • If you enable iCloud sync, data syncs between your own devices via Apple's CloudKit. We cannot access your iCloud data.
  • Daily hexagram and Widget data are shared between the app and widgets via App Groups, stored only on your device.

AI Interpretation Service

  • When you choose "Deep Interpretation", your question and hexagram data are sent to a third-party AI service (DeepSeek) to generate interpretations.
  • We only send hexagram data and the question you actively input. No device identifiers or personal information are attached.
  • The AI service provider has its own privacy policy. Please refer to their terms.

Local Notifications

  • If you enable "Daily Hexagram Reminder", the app uses iOS local notifications to send a daily reminder.
  • Notification content is generated on your device and does not pass through any external servers.

Permissions

  • Motion Sensors — Used only to detect shake gestures for divination interaction. Sensor data is not stored or transmitted.
  • Notifications — Used only for daily hexagram reminders. Can be disabled at any time in Settings.

Third-Party Services

  • This app contains no advertising frameworks or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple's App Store, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Tracking

This app does not track any of your activity. We do not use IDFA or any device identifiers for tracking.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact: jiamanjemma@gmail.com
